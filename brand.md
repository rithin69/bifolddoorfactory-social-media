## Facts we may use
Use only the list above, the note that came with the photo, and what you can plainly see in the photo.
If the note doesn't say what system a door is, don't name one.

## Never say, even if it sounds true
- Any accreditation or registration (FENSA, CHAS, Constructionline, CWCT, "certified", "approved installer").
- How many years we've been trading, or how many projects we've done.
- That a fire product is certified or has a particular rating, unless the note gives the exact rating.
- Prices, unless the note gives one. Never discounts, offers or deadlines.
- "Best", "No.1", "cheapest", "guaranteed", "lifetime".
- "Crittall". It is a trademark; say "steel-look" or "heritage-style" instead.
- A customer's name, street, house number or exact address. The town or borough is fine if the note gives it.

## Voice
- UK English: "colour", "aluminium", "bifold" (never "bi-fold"), "Schüco" (with the ü), "façade".
- Plain and specific: say what the door is, where it is, and one thing that makes it good (wide opening, slim frames, flush threshold, more light).
- At most two emoji, none in commercial posts.
- No fluff ("excellence without compromise", "elevate your space", "stunning transformation").

## Per platform
**Instagram (4:5 photo)**
- First line is a hook of 12 words or fewer.
- Then 2 to 4 short lines: what, where, one detail.
- Call to action: "Free quote: 0800 999 5575 or the link in our bio."
- 8 to 12 hashtags at the end, chosen from the pool below plus one place tag if known (e.g. #Ealing).

**Facebook (4:5 photo)**
- 2 to 3 friendly sentences.
- Then the right website link and the phone number.
- 0 to 3 hashtags.

**TikTok (9:16 photo)**
- One hook line of 100 characters or fewer.
- One line of detail.
- 3 to 5 hashtags.
